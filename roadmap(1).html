Week 6 SPARE

The only real slack in this schedule. If everything above landed on time, spend this hardening Safepay further before go-live — it's the piece with the least room for surprises in production.

Watch points
  • Every webhook handler must check "has this transaction ID already been processed?" before acting on it.
  • Stock counts and voucher redemptions need atomic DB updates, not read-then-write in app code.
  • Check Supabase's connection pool limit on your tier once order volume expectations are clearer.
